UNDER 10% IS THE DANGEROUS END — THAT'S WHERE SWAY STARTS, AND IT GETS WORSE WITH SPEED. OVER ABOUT 15% YOU START UNLOADING THE TOW VEHICLE'S FRONT AXLE AND LOSING STEERING. HOLD 10-15% OF GROSS.

EACH PART'S MASS CENTER IS ASSUMED AT MID-LENGTH. CLOSE ENOUGH FOR BOXES AND TANKS; A KAYAK OR LOADED RACK CAN SIT OFF-CENTER, SO NUDGE THE FRONT POSITION IF YOU KNOW BETTER.

SET AXLE ASSEMBLY WT AND THE AXLE'S OWN MASS IS MODELLED RIDING AT THE AXLE. IT CARRIES ENTIRELY ON ITSELF, SO IT ADDS NOTHING TO TONGUE WEIGHT WHILE STILL COUNTING IN GROSS — WHICH PUSHES THE TARGET POSITION AFT. A 200 LB AXLE UNDER 830 LB OF LOAD MOVES IT ABOUT 3.8". DO NOT ALSO COUNT THE AXLE IN THE FRAME WEIGHT.

TORSION AXLES CARRY THE LOAD AT THE WHEEL, NOT THE BRACKET. THE TRAILING ARM PUTS THE WHEEL CENTER ROUGHLY 4.4-6.0" AFT OF THE TORSION BAR ON A 6" ARM, AND THAT REACH CHANGES AS THE TRAILER LOADS AND THE ARM SWINGS UP. SET LOCATE AXLE BY TO TORSION BRACKET AND IT IS WORKED OUT FOR YOU.

TANDEM AND TRIPLE TORSION AXLES DO NOT EQUALIZE. THERE IS NO EQUALIZER BETWEEN THEM THE WAY LEAF SPRINGS HAVE ONE, SO THE PER-AXLE SPLIT BELOW IS NOMINAL. UNEVEN RIDE HEIGHT OR AN OUT-OF-LEVEL TRAILER PUTS MORE ON ONE AXLE THAN THE OTHER. THE GROUP IS STILL BALANCED AS ONE SUPPORT AT THE GROUP CENTER FOR TONGUE WEIGHT.

PRESET WEIGHTS ARE TYPICAL STARTING NUMBERS — WEIGH THE REAL PART WHEN IT MATTERS.
