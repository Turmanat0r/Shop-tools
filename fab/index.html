A36 steel, allowable bending 0.6×Fy = 21.6 ksi, allowable shear 0.4×Fy = 14.4 ksi, E = 29,000 ksi. Section properties model radiused corners at an outside radius of 2×wall, reproducing the published AISC HSS values for I, S and weight — a sharp-corner area runs 4-11% heavy, not light. Single member, laterally braced. Uniform loads are the total load over the span, not per-foot. Capacity is the load you add — the tube’s own weight is not subtracted.

The dynamic factor divides static capacity for impact and road shock — a 500 lb load hitting a pothole can momentarily push two or three times that through the frame. Size to the rated number, and if you're near the line, go up a wall thickness.
