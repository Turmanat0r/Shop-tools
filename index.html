A36 steel, allowable bending stress 0.6×Fy = 21.6 ksi, allowable shear 0.4×Fy = 14.4 ksi, E = 29,000 ksi. Section properties model radiused corners at an outside radius of 2×wall, which reproduces the published AISC HSS values for I, S and weight. Single member only, laterally braced, load applied about the axis shown. Uniform loads are the total load spread over the span, not per-foot. Capacity is the load you add — the tube's own weight is not subtracted, so on long spans of heavy wall, deduct it yourself.

Cantilever deflection is checked against the limit over the projection itself (L/limit), which is stricter than the common practice of allowing twice that on a cantilever — conservative on purpose.

The dynamic factor divides static capacity to account for impact and road shock — a 500 lb load hitting a pothole can momentarily push two or three times that through the frame. Rated load is what you can actually put on the member in that service condition. These multipliers are common shop practice, not code values; for a member carrying axle or hitch loads on a road-going trailer, size to the rated number and treat anything close to the line as a call for the next wall thickness up.
