EACH STOCK TYPE NESTS SEPARATELY — A 2×2 TUBE CUT CANNOT COME OFF A LENGTH OF PIPE. THE NESTER RUNS FIRST-FIT AND BEST-FIT PASSES PER STOCK TYPE AND REPORTS WHICHEVER CONSUMES FEWER STICKS; ON A TIE IT FAVORS THE NEST LEAVING ONE LONG REMNANT OVER SEVERAL SHORT ONES.

KERF IS CHARGED BETWEEN ADJACENT PIECES AND AGAIN TO FREE THE REMNANT — N PIECES PLUS A DROP MEANS N CUTS, NOT N−1. A REMNANT SHORTER THAN ONE KERF IS REPORTED AS NOTHING, BECAUSE THE BLADE EATS IT. REMNANTS CLEARING THE SALVAGE THRESHOLD ARE FLAGGED GREEN AND WORTH RACKING.

MASS IS A36 AT 0.2836 LB/IN³. SQUARE AND RECT TUBE USE RADIUSED CORNERS AT 2×WALL, WHICH REPRODUCES THE PUBLISHED AISC HSS WEIGHTS — A SHARP-CORNER AREA RUNS 4-6% HEAVY. PIPE AND ROUND TUBE ARE EXACT. ANGLE IGNORES THE FILLET AND READS ABOUT 0.2% LIGHT.
